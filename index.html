How to play

Angry Canada geese pop out from behind the trees, bushes and rocks by the lake. Knock them out of the sky with your slingshot. Each round lasts 60 seconds.

  • Pull the slingshot down and let go. Pull further for a higher, longer shot; pull to the side to aim left or right.
  • Watch for a shaking bush or tree: a goose is about to fly out of it.
  • Far-away geese are smaller and worth more points. Hit geese in a row for a combo.
  • Hits fill the fever gauge. When it's full, fever time sends a whole flock your way.
  • Every round uses one slingshot. Out of slingshots? Wait for free ones or watch a short video.
